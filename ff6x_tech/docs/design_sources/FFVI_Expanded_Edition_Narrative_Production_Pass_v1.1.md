# FINAL FANTASY VI — EXPANDED EDITION
## Narrative Production Pass v1.1

**Status:** canonical expansion of `Scenario Script v1.0`.

`Scenario Script v1.0` established the emotional spine and key dialogue scenes. It was **not long enough to be the final production script** for eight major arcs. The problem was not that individual conversations were too short — FFVI works best with concise lines — but that several arcs did not yet contain enough *beats between* the major conversations.

The production rule from v1.1 onward is therefore:

- **Do not make speeches longer.**
- **Add more situations, reactions, exploration dialogue, NPC-state changes and quiet scenes.**
- Major arcs should normally contain **12–15 meaningful story beats**, not 6–8.
- A major arc should feel like **30–60 minutes of optional FFVI content**, including exploration and combat, without becoming a second main story.
- Minor reconstruction quests stay short.
- Final ROM line breaks and text-box splitting are deferred until text-bank audit.

The dialogue below is additive to v1.0 and is written in intended story order.

# DIALOGUE LENGTH / ROM IMPLEMENTATION GUIDELINES

## What “full” means for this mod

A full script does **not** mean every scene should be long. FFVI's strongest moments often use very few lines. “Full” means the game contains enough connective tissue that characters do not teleport emotionally from setup to resolution.

### Target density by content type

| Content type | Target story beats | Approx. dialogue boxes | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| WOB seed | 2–5 | 10–30 | foreshadow only |
| Major WoR arc | 12–15 | 70–125 | includes NPC states and optional party lines |
| Forgotten Age dungeon | 6–8 each | 30–50 each | evidence + character reaction + gameplay |
| First Magi finale | 12–15 | 90–130 | spread across dungeon, not one monologue |
| Minor reconstruction quest | 3–4 | 8–20 | deliberately concise |
| Ending vignette | 1 | 3–10 | visual storytelling first |

### Hard style rules

1. **Never solve an emotional arc with one speech.** Use actions and revisits.
2. **Never explain a visual that the map already communicates.**
3. **Never turn Kefka into a misunderstood victim.** Records explain process, not morality.
4. **Never resurrect canonical dead characters to create drama.** Memory is enough.
5. **Shadow speaks less than everyone else.** Relm forces the scene forward.
6. **Terra's philosophy comes from concrete acts: water, doors, crops, children.**
7. **Celes speaks clinically when hurt; emotion leaks through what she refuses to say.**
8. **Cyan's archaic English stays readable and should loosen slightly around trusted friends.**
9. **Edgar uses humor to conceal burden; Sabin challenges the concealment rather than the kingship itself.**
10. **Setzer jokes when cornered, then becomes very plain when the truth lands.**
11. **Strago is lore authority but is allowed to be wrong or incomplete.** Ancient records can contradict him.
12. **Relm punctures solemnity, but never destroys a serious scene merely for a joke.**
13. **NPCs should sound like people who need food, roofs, work and family — not lore encyclopedias.**
14. **Hope Flags alter scenes, not the moral worth of the player.**
15. **The vanilla ending remains valid if every expansion arc is skipped.**
